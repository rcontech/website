# Modernizing Consent and Marketing Measurement Across a National Healthcare Network

> **INTERNAL CASE STUDY OUTLINE — NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLICATION.** Do not interpret a technically successful rollout as proof of regulatory compliance.

**Healthcare | Marketing Infrastructure | Consent Management | Change Leadership**

## Business challenge
Coordinate a consent-management and measurement architecture transition across a large multi-location healthcare network while maintaining meaningful marketing reporting.

## Scope and role
- National network of approximately 400+ locations (confirm exact scope at time of rollout)
- Coordinated vendors, legal, operations and marketing stakeholders
- Worked on consent configuration, tag categorization, rollout sequencing and validation

## Approach
1. Audit tags, events and consent categorization.
2. Pilot a limited set of clinics and geographic consent scenarios.
3. Validate intended behavior across measurement paths and vendors.
4. Extend rollout in phases and conduct post-launch health checks.

## Working outcome
Launch was reported as having **no critical failures**. Verify incident logs, dates and specific system health criteria before publication.

## Business lesson
Marketing transformation depends as much on governance, careful testing and organizational coordination as on the technology itself.

## Evidence to assemble
Go/no-go notes, test cases, vendor acceptance, consent-state tests, post-launch validation reports, approved compliance language, client naming permission.
