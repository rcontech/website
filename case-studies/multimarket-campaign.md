# Coordinating a Multimarket Patient Acquisition Initiative

> **INTERNAL CASE STUDY OUTLINE — NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLICATION.** Investment size is scope, not an outcome.

**Healthcare | Enterprise Growth | Agency Leadership | Market Expansion**

## Business challenge
Coordinate a major patient acquisition initiative across markets with differing competitive environments and acquisition priorities.

## Scope
- Approximately $7 million in campaign investment
- Ten metropolitan markets
- Enterprise coordination between marketing leadership and an external agency

## Approach
- Align program objectives with location and market priorities.
- Coordinate partners and execution decisions.
- Establish consistent performance review and budget governance.
- Support cross-market decisions and escalation.

## Results still needed
Quantified incremental patients/appointments, comparable demand growth, cost per acquisition, return on investment, or market-specific performance; dates and roles should also be documented.

## Business lesson
Multimarket growth efforts require a shared decision framework without assuming every market needs identical execution.

## Evidence to assemble
Approved budgets, campaign timeline, market list, performance reporting, allocation changes, executive reviews, permission to disclose scope.
