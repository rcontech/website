# Doubling Reported Lead Volume While Reducing Paid Media Spending

> **INTERNAL DRAFT — NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLICATION.** Lead tracking and conversion definitions changed; do not claim doubled *incremental customer demand* or improved lead quality until comparable metrics are confirmed.

## At a Glance
- **Organization:** Multi-location urgent care provider (AFC Urgent Care — name for internal reference only pending permission)
- **Scope:** Approximately 90 locations
- **Period:** January–December 2024
- **Reported monthly lead volume:** Approximately 6,000 to 12,000
- **Monthly paid media spending:** Approximately $180,000 to $130,000
- **Calculated change in spending:** 27.8% decrease

## The Challenge
Managing paid acquisition across a large network of healthcare locations requires more than allocating budget across campaigns.

Each location operates within its own competitive environment, while enterprise leadership needs a consistent way to understand marketing performance and make investment decisions.

The opportunity was to improve acquisition efficiency while maintaining visibility into the leads being generated across the network.

## The Approach

### Improving conversion measurement
Reviewing and refining campaign conversion goals to better reflect meaningful customer actions.

### Strengthening response pathways
Enhancing measurement and opportunities around phone calls and text-based inquiries.

### Optimizing media investment
Evaluating campaign performance and reallocating spending to improve efficiency across participating locations.

### Connecting execution to business priorities
Coordinating performance improvements within the broader goals of the multi-location acquisition program.

## The Results
By December 2024, reported monthly lead volume had increased from approximately 6,000 to 12,000, while monthly paid media spending had decreased from approximately $180,000 to $130,000.

That represents:
- Approximately 100% higher *reported* monthly lead volume
- Approximately 27.8% lower monthly media spending
- A mathematical change in reported leads per media dollar **that must not be marketed as a comparable efficiency improvement until measurement definitions are reconciled**

**Important measurement context:** Changes to conversion definitions and tracking can affect reported lead volume. Confirm which metrics are comparable and distinguish measurement improvements from incremental customer demand.

## The Business Lesson
Marketing efficiency is not always improved by spending more.

Sometimes the greatest opportunity comes from understanding which customer actions matter, improving the paths to conversion, and making investment decisions with better information.

For multi-location organizations, that requires both operational discipline and a broader view of how acquisition performance supports business growth.
