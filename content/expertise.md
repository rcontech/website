# Growth Requires More Than Expertise in One Channel.

The most consequential growth challenges often sit between functions: marketing and sales, strategy and execution, customer experience and technology, or data and business decisions.

R Consulting helps organizations connect those disciplines.

## Growth Strategy & Acquisition
Build a clearer path from market opportunity to measurable business performance.

Our work includes acquisition strategy, channel investment, conversion improvement, lifecycle marketing, expansion planning, and customer economics.

**Business outcome:** More effective investment in the activities that create valuable customers.

## Customer Experience & Conversion
Understand what customers actually experience as they move from awareness to engagement, purchase, enrollment, or appointment.

We evaluate journey design, operational handoffs, conversion barriers, and opportunities to improve the experience.

**Business outcome:** Less friction between customer interest and business results.

## Data, Analytics & Marketing Technology
Your reporting should help answer business questions—not create more confusion.

We work across marketing measurement, CRM and CDP strategy, analytics, attribution, data quality, privacy-conscious technology decisions, and platform integration planning.

**Business outcome:** More trustworthy information and a technology environment aligned with business priorities.

## Executive Leadership & Marketing Operations
Translate strategy into decisions, accountability, and coordinated execution.

We support marketing planning, organizational design, budget management, vendor oversight, cross-functional alignment, and performance governance.

**Business outcome:** A marketing organization better equipped to execute and scale.

## Team Enablement & Transformation

**Business outcome:** More sustainable performance with less dependence on outside resources.
