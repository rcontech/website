# Frequently Asked Questions

## What types of organizations does R Consulting work with?
We work with organizations that need experienced support navigating growth, acquisition performance, marketing leadership, or technology-related challenges. Our experience is especially relevant to healthcare, technology-enabled businesses, and organizations operating in complex environments.

## Do you work with businesses that already have marketing teams?
Yes. Many engagements involve supporting an existing team, strengthening leadership capacity, improving coordination, or helping an organization tackle a challenge outside its current capabilities.

## What's the difference between strategic advisory and fractional leadership?
Strategic advisory focuses on evaluating challenges, guiding decisions, and establishing priorities. Fractional leadership involves taking a more active role in directing marketing initiatives, coordinating teams, and supporting ongoing execution and accountability.

## Can you help with a single project?
Yes. Focused engagements are available for clearly defined challenges such as acquisition performance, customer journey improvement, measurement, or marketing technology assessments.

## Do you replace our existing marketing agency?
Not necessarily. We often work alongside existing agencies and internal teams, helping establish priorities, improve coordination, and connect their work to broader business objectives.

## Do you implement marketing technology?
Depending on the engagement, we can help assess requirements, develop strategy, coordinate vendors, guide implementation, and validate outcomes. The scope of direct implementation support is determined by the project.

## Do you provide training for internal teams?
Yes. Training and enablement can be incorporated into engagements to support new processes, marketing systems, reporting practices, and organizational capabilities.

## How long do engagements last?
Engagement length depends on the problem and level of support required. A focused assessment may take several weeks, while advisory and fractional leadership relationships may continue over multiple months.

## How do we get started?
Start with a conversation about your business objectives, current challenges, and internal resources. We'll help determine the most useful next step.
